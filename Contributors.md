## Third-party work

- The weather alert receiver's voice and tone (made with the Piper speech engine): see
  [doc/Credits-WeatherVoice.md](doc/Credits-WeatherVoice.md).
- [JOrbis](http://www.jcraft.com/jorbis/) (LGPL) decodes tape recordings (.ogg) on the server; it is bundled,
  relocated, in the mod jar.
- Built on [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/), with [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrepo.de/minecraft/voicechat/) for
  all radio audio, and optional support for Patchouli, Curios, Jade, The One Probe, JEI, Create and Sable.
