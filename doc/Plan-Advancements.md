# Advancements batch (decided 2026-11-06, maintainer)

This batch starts after "Computer batch 3C" (Net Control, awards, QSL cards). Afterwards: runData, test, then launch the client for a playtest.

Today the mod has only two advancements: `going_ham` and `found_secret_floppy`. Build all of the advancements below **except "Up the Tower" and "Off the Grid"** (skipped by the maintainer).

## Shared rule: a verified two-way contact
A and B have a verified contact when A heard B and B heard A within 5 minutes. "Heard" uses the moderation log's transmitter and hearer data. FT8 contacts the mod completed also count.

This one check drives First Contact, Contest Station, Skip Day, DX Hunter, Grey Line and Moonbounce. Hand-typed logbook entries do **not** count.

## The list
**Getting started**
- Going Ham (exists).
- Is This Thing On? Your voice reached another player's radio while that player was close enough to hear it.
- First Contact: your first verified contact.
- Licensed to Chill: set a callsign with `/callsign`.
- Ground Plane Theory: build your first antenna.

**Distance and propagation**
- Skip Day: a verified contact over 1,000 blocks on HF.
- DX Hunter: a verified contact over 5,000 blocks.
- Grey Line: a verified contact at dawn or dusk.
- Into the Static: a contact during a solar storm or blackout.
- Northern Lights: hear a signal during an aurora.
- Moonbounce *(hidden)*: a verified contact with both stations above Y 200.

**Modes**
- Dit Dah: send CW.
- Typewriter Thunder: copy RTTY.
- Weak Signal Wizard: complete an FT8 contact.
- Picture This: receive an SSTV picture.
- Chart Topper: print a WEFAX chart.
- Packet Rat: post to another station's Packet BBS.

**Station building**
- Repeater Owner: another player's transmission is relayed by a repeater you placed. Repeaters must record who placed them.
- Shack Upgrade: own a radio computer.
- Mission Control: a computer with a monitor, scanner, radio interface and speakers.
- Digital Native: install 10 programs.

**Commercial and public service**
- Fleet Manager: program a radio with the CPS by hand.
- Clone Wars: use the Radio Cloner.
- Out of Band *(hidden)*: fit a MARS mod.
- Storm Spotter: your Weather Alert Generator broadcasts its first warning.
- Take Shelter Now: a receiver sounds a tornado warning, then a real tornado comes within about 100 blocks of it within a few minutes. It goes to players near the receiver.

**Logging and community**
- Net Control: run a net with 5 or more check-ins.
- Paper Trail: another player picks up, or holds, a QSL card you signed.
- Card Collector: a QSL album holding 20 cards.
- Worked All Biomes: tied to the awards tracker's award.
- Contest Station: 100 verified contacts.

**Secret and fun** *(hidden)*
- Bible Study, High Score (Pileup) and Spin the Disk (play a tune): one per secret floppy program.
- Numbers Station: hear a numbers station broadcast.
- The FCC Is Watching: be given an FCC badge.
