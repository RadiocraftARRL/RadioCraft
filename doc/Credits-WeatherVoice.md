# Credits: the weather alert voice and tone

The Simple Weather Alert Receiver (1.12) announces alerts by splicing pre-made word clips, as the real automated
weather radio voice does. The clips and the attention tone were made once, on 2026-11-06, by `tools/weather_voice.py`,
and are shipped as Ogg Vorbis files in `src/main/resources/assets/radiocraft/sounds/weather_alert/`
(`tone.ogg` and `word_<word>.ogg`, 64 files). Nothing in them stops them being shipped in RadioCraft (GPLv3).

## The voice

| What | Where | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Speech engine: **Piper** (`piper-tts` 1.8.0 on PyPI) | https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl | GPL-3.0 (the engine; its output is not covered by it) |
| Voice model: **en_US-ljspeech-high** | https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high | MIT (the piper-voices repository), trained from scratch by Bryce Beattie (https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/) |
| Training data: **LJ Speech Dataset** (Keith Ito and Linda Johnson, 2017) | https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/ | Public domain (the model card's "Dataset License: public domain") |

The model card was checked on 2026-11-06. The voice is a US English female voice. "RadioCraft" is spoken as "Radio
craft"; no real agency or service is named in any clip.

## The tone

`tone.ogg` is a plain 1050 Hz sine for 5 seconds with 5 ms fades, generated by the script with numpy (no recording),
after the attention tone real weather radios sound before an alert.

## Making them again

```
python -m venv venv && venv/Scripts/python -m pip install piper-tts numpy soundfile
(download en_US-ljspeech-high.onnx and en_US-ljspeech-high.onnx.json from the piper-voices link above)
venv/Scripts/python tools/weather_voice.py --model path/to/en_US-ljspeech-high.onnx
```

The script prints each clip's length as `WeatherVoiceClip` enum entries
(`common/radio/wxalert/WeatherVoiceClip.java`); paste them in if the words or the voice change, and add any new word to
`sounds.json` (`weather_alert.word_<word>`). Another voice may be used only if its model **and** its training data
allow redistribution (many Piper voices are trained on data under non-commercial or unclear licences: check the
model card's dataset licence, not just the repository's).
